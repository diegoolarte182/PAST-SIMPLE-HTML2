Past Simple

Think of a photo: a completed event in the past.

subject + past verb
I visited Bogotá yesterday.
🎬

Past Continuous

Think of a movie: an action was happening at a moment in the past.

was / were + verb-ing
I was visiting Bogotá at 5 p.m.

🎬 The classic combination

🚶
I was walking home when it started to rain.

was walking = action in progress (background)

started = completed event that happened during it
